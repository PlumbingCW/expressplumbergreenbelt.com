Sewage backup or no hot water: first steps in Lanham

These are the two calls we get most from Lanham. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for both.

  • For a backup, stop using water and keep children and pets away from the affected drain.
  • For a failed water heater, close the cold valve above it and switch off its power or gas control.
  • For any leak, close the main valve and photograph the damage.
  • Call the 24/7 line. A plumber is dispatched from Greenbelt.

If you smell gas near a heater, leave the house and call your gas utility or 911 before calling us.

Why Lanham houses fail in groups

Lanham and Seabrook were counted together as Lanham-Seabrook until the 2010 census split them into two places. In 2000 that combined area held 6,468 housing units in about 5.3 square miles, mostly suburban houses laid out in the same decades.

Streets built in the same years tend to have appliances installed in the same years. When one water heater on a block fails at fifteen years, its neighbors are close behind. If yours is old, ask about water heater replacement before the cold morning it quits.

New Carrollton and Seabrook run right up against Lanham, so a plumber who knows one knows all three.

Basements and storms

Many suburban homes in the area have a basement or a low crawl space, and the ground around them holds water after a heavy storm. A sump pump that fails during a power cut is a classic Lanham call, which is why we offer battery backup.

If your basement is damp but not flooding, a drainage plan may fix it for less than a full system. See basement waterproofing for how we decide.

Planning ahead for the water heater

A tank in a Lanham basement or utility closet rarely fails without warning. Rumbling, rust at the drain valve and puddles near the base all show up weeks before the leak. A flush or an anode check can extend the life of a tank that is still sound.

If the tank is past ten years and shows any of those signs, replace it on your schedule instead of the tank's. A planned swap lets you choose the size, fuel and warranty. See water heater installation for the choices and the price factors.

We bring common sizes to emergency calls, so a same-day replacement is often possible when stock allows.

Roots and aging laterals in suburban yards

Lanham yards have large trees, and roots enter older sewer laterals at joints. The first sign is a slow drain that clears for a while after cabling and then returns. A camera shows whether roots, a belly or a cracked pipe is behind it.

Clearing roots restores flow for a time, but only a lining or repair stops them. We show you the footage so the decision is yours.

Codes that apply to Lanham and Seabrook work

Lanham and Seabrook are in WSSC territory, so the plumbing code WSSC Water enforces applies, along with its permit and inspection process. Gas fitting follows NFPA 54, the National Fuel Gas Code, and must be done by a licensed professional.

For drainage, the County's Residential Drainage Manual is the local guideline. It recommends sump outfalls 8 to 10 feet from the foundation and a call to DPIE before drainage work.

Lanham ZIP codes and service notes

Lanham uses the 20706 ZIP code, with 20703 used mainly for post office boxes. Dispatch confirms the street address on the call, because some streets near Seabrook and New Carrollton use a neighboring ZIP.

WSSC Water supplies the area and issues plumbing permits. WSSC says to ask a plumber for a license number and check it by phone. We handle the permit and inspection on work that requires one.
