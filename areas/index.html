Greenbelt, our home base

Our dispatch is based in Greenbelt, so Old Greenbelt, Springhill Lake, the Roosevelt Center area and the neighborhoods along Greenbelt Road get the fastest response. Our emergency plumber page for Greenbelt covers the city itself.

Greenbelt uses ZIP codes 20770, 20771 and 20768. Each nearby town page lists its own ZIP codes, so you can confirm yours at a glance.

How dispatch works across towns

When you call, we ask for your street address first. That tells us which town you are in and which plumber is closest. A plumber leaves our Greenbelt base with the common parts for the problem you describe, and we give you an arrival estimate on the phone.

Estimates depend on traffic on Greenbelt Road, the Baltimore-Washington Parkway and the Beltway, and on how many emergency calls are open. For anything that cannot wait, call (301) 936-0389 and we will tell you honestly how soon we can be there.

ZIP codes we cover

Greenbelt is served by 20770, 20771 and 20768. College Park and Berwyn Heights share 20740, and College Park also uses 20742 on the University of Maryland campus. Lanham is 20706, Beltsville is 20705 and Hyattsville uses 20781 through 20785. Open a town page to see how its ZIP codes map to local streets.

Other places we reach

Seabrook and New Carrollton are served from the same dispatch as Lanham. Riverdale Park is close to College Park, and Landover Hills is next to Hyattsville. Bowie and Glenn Dale lie further out. Call (301) 936-0389 and we will tell you honestly whether we can arrive in time for an emergency.
