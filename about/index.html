What we do

Greenbelt Express Plumbing is a Greenbelt-based plumbing service. We answer a 24/7 emergency line and send licensed plumbers to homes and businesses in Greenbelt and the nearby Prince George's County towns. We handle drains, water heaters, leaks, sewer lines, repiping and more.

The business address is 7500 Greenway Center Dr, Greenbelt, MD 20770. We take phone calls, not online forms, because a plumbing problem is easier to solve in a conversation.

How we work

Every job starts with a conversation. You describe the problem, we ask a few questions and we tell you whether it is an emergency, what to do while you wait and how the price works. For repairs, you hear the price before work begins.

We write down what we find, explain the options in plain language and leave decisions to you. If a problem is not ours to fix, such as a gas leak or a public sewer main, we say so and point you to the right party.

Licensing and permits

In Prince George's County, WSSC Water says most plumbing must be done by a WSSC Water-licensed plumber or gasfitter, and permits are applied for through its ePermitting system. WSSC suggests asking for a license number and verifying it by calling 301-206-8588.

We follow the plumbing and fuel gas codes the county enforces, and we handle permits and inspections for the work we do. We encourage you to run the same license check on any contractor.

Where we work

We serve Greenbelt, including Old Greenbelt and Springhill Lake, and the nearby places of College Park, Lanham, Beltsville, Berwyn Heights and Hyattsville. See the areas we serve for details.

The calls we get most

Most of our calls fall into a handful of groups. Drains and sewer backups come first, then water heaters that fail or leak, then hidden leaks and burst pipes. Planned work such as repiping, sump pumps and fixture installs makes up the rest.

That mix shapes what we carry on the truck. Plumbers arrive with drain machines, common water heater parts, pipe fittings for copper and plastic and leak-finding equipment, so many repairs finish in one visit. When a part has to be ordered, we say so on the phone.

Housing we know

Greenbelt is a planned town from the 1930s, and Greenbelt Homes, Inc. describes its older homes as block, brick and frame buildings, many without basements. Nearby towns add Victorian houses in Hyattsville and Berwyn Heights, student rentals in College Park and post-war suburban homes in Lanham and Beltsville.

Each type fails in a different way. Old galvanized supply lines lose pressure, clay sewer laterals collect roots, and tanks that run all day in rentals wear out early. Knowing the housing helps us ask the right questions on the phone and bring the right parts.

Pricing and honesty

We do not publish flat prices, because the same symptom can have very different causes. Instead we explain what drives the cost, give you the number before work starts and tell you what is included. If a cheaper fix will do, we recommend it.

We write down what we find so you can show it to a landlord, an insurer or a second plumber. If you want another opinion, that is reasonable, and WSSC Water suggests getting estimates from more than one plumber for sewer work.

Safety comes before the repair

Before any repair, we think about what could hurt you. Water near electricity, sewage in living space, a failing gas appliance and a tank that could rupture all come ahead of the plumbing fix. On the phone we tell you what to switch off, what to avoid and when to leave the building.

After a sewage backup we advise gloves, boots and keeping children and pets out of the area, and after a flood we advise photographing damage before cleanup. These small steps protect your health and support an insurance claim.

Codes and standards

Plumbing work in this part of Maryland follows the plumbing and fuel gas rules that WSSC Water enforces, and permitted work is inspected. Inspectors need an adult to let them in, so plan to be home or send a representative.

We cite those rules where they matter on our pages, and we say plainly when we are unsure. If a page here conflicts with what WSSC, the County or the City of Greenbelt says, follow them.

What we do not do

We do not respond to gas leaks. If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911. We do not do water damage restoration, and we do not fix public water or sewer mains, which belong to WSSC Water. WSSC's emergency line for those is 301-206-4002.
