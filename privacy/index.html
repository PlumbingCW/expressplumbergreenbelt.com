Privacy Policy

How we handle information from our phone line and this website.

Overview

This policy describes how Greenbelt Express Plumbing handles information when you visit this website or call our phone line. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Phone calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number, address and the details of your plumbing problem so that we can send a plumber and follow up on the job.

This website

This site does not collect information through online forms. Our hosting provider may log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested to keep the site secure and working. We do not sell this information.

How we use information

We use the information you give us on a call to schedule and complete your service, to improve our service and to meet legal obligations. We share it with the plumbers and dispatch staff who need it to do your job.

Cookies

This site is designed to work without tracking cookies. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this page.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing contact@expressplumbergreenbelt.com or calling (301) 936-0389.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version on this page.
